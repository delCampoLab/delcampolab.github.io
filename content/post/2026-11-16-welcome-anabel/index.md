We are very happy to welcome [Anabel Espinar](/author/anabel-espinar/) to the del Campo Lab as our new Lab Manager and Laboratory Technician! 🧪🦠

<!--more-->

Anabel trained in Clinical and Biomedical Laboratory Science at IES Les Vinyes and had her first taste of research during an internship at Vall d'Hebron University Hospital. She then joined the Centre for Genomic Regulation (CRG) as a Laboratory Technician, where she helped develop and optimize laboratory protocols and gained hands-on experience in DNA extraction and sequencing, especially with Oxford Nanopore. Along the way she has also worked with microalgae, maintaining and monitoring cultures and running molecular and biochemical techniques such as ELISA and Western blotting.

Here at IBE, she will focus on developing and optimizing new protocols for the lab, supporting the research team, and making sure everything in the lab runs smoothly. Her experience with long-read sequencing and with microalgal cultures is a great fit for our work on the microbes living with corals and other marine animals.

Welcome aboard, Anabel! We are thrilled to have you with us! 🎉
