A collection of resources we find useful and share with students, visitors, and anyone curious about marine microbial ecology. 🪸🦠

- [PhD Fellowships](/resources/phd-fellowships/): funding schemes to do a PhD with us.
- [Postdoc Fellowships](/resources/postdoc-fellowships/): fellowships to join the lab as a postdoc.
- [Tools](/resources/tools/): databases and software we use and contribute to.
- [Journals](/resources/journals/): where our community publishes.
- [Societies](/resources/societies/): scientific societies we are part of.
- [Friends](/resources/friends/): labs and groups we collaborate with.
